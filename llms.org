* Conventions

- ~dev-nixify~ is a single POSIX-sh script.  Keep it POSIX (no bashisms);
  ~nix develop~ provides ~shellcheck~ and ~shfmt~ for linting and formatting.
- Prose (org, comments, commit bodies) uses two spaces after
  sentence-ending punctuation.
- The injected devShell flake follows the rust-template idiom
  (~forAllSystems~ / ~flakeExposed~ / ~perSystem~), trimmed to a plain
  ~devShell~.  If that idiom changes upstream, mirror it in ~write_flake~.
